# Ravoq working identity

Ravoq is the working name used in the application. This package is implemented original artwork, not a claim of trademark/domain clearance or professional cultural endorsement.

## Files and use

- `public/branding/logo/ravoq-primary-v1.svg`: horizontal lockup, minimum width 122px.
- `ravoq-stacked-v1.svg`: compact lockup, minimum width 85px.
- `ravoq-mark-v1.svg`: icon, minimum 24px for the detailed mark.
- `ravoq-monochrome-v1.svg`: one-color output; `ravoq-reversed-v1.svg` for forest backgrounds; `ravoq-light-v1.svg` for cream surfaces.
- App favicon uses a simplified arch at small sizes. All SVG marks use paths rather than external fonts, scripts or links.
- Leave at least half the arch width clear on all sides. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor with low-contrast combinations, or embed the mark into QR modules.

The app wordmark uses the local system font; exported wordmark paths are independently drawn for font-independent print output. Both share the arch and forest/cream identity. Body typography is system sans; editorial headings use available Georgia/serif with system fallback. No remote font request or bundled third-party font license is involved.

Plate illustrations are original stylized art, not photographs of a real restaurant's food. Replace them with a restaurant's validated images for live menus. The geometric tile is a contemporary motif inspired by broad geometric ornament, with no claim to reproduce a specific historic object.

`manifest.json` records source, size, checksum and review status. QR sheets come from authenticated per-restaurant print routes with real generated codes; they are not static decorative assets in this package.

Shortlist: **Ravoq**, **Ravon**, **Taoma**, **Chinni**, **Mavsum**. Ravoq best connects the existing arch mark to a flexible platform that can host many restaurant identities. The working choice permits implementation; final business clearance remains with the owner.

Tagline options: “Dasturxoningizga raqamli qiyofa.” / “Menyudan mehmondo‘stlikkacha.” / “Har bir taomga munosib taqdimot.” The shipped English hero is “Good food. Beautifully presented.”

Voice: warm, concise and useful. Prefer concrete menu/customer actions over claims of guaranteed growth or authenticity. Cultural ornament remains optional and away from prices, controls and QR quiet zones.
